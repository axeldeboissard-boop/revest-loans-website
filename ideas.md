# Revest Loans — Reference-Led Recreation

## Ground-Truth Specification

The supplied `revest-loans.zip` archive is the authoritative reference for this recreation. The rebuilt website will recover and preserve the source site’s content hierarchy, conversion paths, visual character, navigation, and available interaction patterns. Any changes will be limited to compatibility, responsive behavior, accessibility, and presentation consistency needed to make the recreated project practical to maintain in this workspace.

## Chosen Design Philosophy: Trusted Property Finance

**Design Movement:** Refined editorial financial services with architectural modernism. The visual system positions lending as calm, structured, and property-aware rather than aggressive or sales-led.

**Core Principles:** Information earns confidence through deliberate hierarchy; warmth is supplied through real property and renovation imagery; financial actions remain visually direct and easy to scan; all screen sizes retain the same sense of composure.

**Color Philosophy:** Deep navy provides institutional clarity and focus. A single fresh green accent is reserved for action, progress, and value creation, while warm ivory and stone neutrals prevent the experience from feeling clinical.

**Layout Paradigm:** A flowing editorial sequence with a deliberately offset hero, full-bleed visual interludes, and compact informational rails. The primary conversion route stays continuously visible without forcing every section into a centered card grid.

**Signature Elements:** Architectural linework used as a restrained framing motif; a roofline-and-growth-arrow brand mark; crisp navy-to-ivory section transitions with single-note green action elements.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Interaction should make decisions feel simple and considered. Navigation, calculators, and calls to action respond quickly with subtle scale, underline, and color shifts rather than attention-seeking effects.

**Animation:** Use only short, natural opacity and transform transitions, typically 160–240 ms with a decisive ease-out. Content may rise slightly into view on first exposure; all nonessential motion must honor reduced-motion preferences.

**Typography System:** A high-contrast editorial serif for primary headlines pairs with an articulate humanist sans-serif for interface copy. Headline sizes are generous but not decorative; pricing, form labels, and conditions use compact sans-serif rhythm.

**Brand Essence:** Revest Loans brings clear, property-aware finance to owners and investors who need a practical route forward. **Calm, intelligent, grounded.**

**Brand Voice:** Headlines are specific, reassuring, and oriented toward real property decisions; CTAs are plain spoken and purposeful. Example lines: “Finance shaped around the property in front of you.” “Tell us where you are in the project.”

**Wordmark & Logo:** The wordmark pairs Revest’s tailored editorial typography with a compact architectural symbol: two intersecting planes form a roofline, doorway, and rising value arrow. The mark can operate independently for navigation and favicon use.

**Signature Brand Color:** **Revest Green — #54B947.**

## Style Decisions

- The source archive remains the final authority when its concrete content or layout differs from the guiding philosophy above.
- The generated visual assets are reserved for visually prominent contexts where the original archive does not provide an equivalent usable asset.
- No testimonial, rating, or review content will be fabricated during the recreation.
- **Revest Green #54B947** is the only action/value accent; it is used selectively for primary actions, key numerals, and small indicators rather than large promotional fields.
- Every major page includes a property-aware visual or architectural linework moment, using roofline and structural-rule geometry as the recurring signature.
- Editorial asymmetry and informational rails are the default; repeated card grids are used only where distinct options or comparisons genuinely need them.
